  Code: src/build_manifest.py asserts this for the full dataset.

**Patient ID normalisation:**
  JSON uses both 'MR-XXXXXX' (with hyphen, 3 patients) and 'MRXXXXXX' (no hyphen, 495 patients).
  Image filenames similarly mixed. After stripping hyphens → 498 unique IDs, zero overlap.
  Conclusion: these are the SAME naming convention with inconsistent hyphens.
  Code: norm_patient_id() strips hyphens + spaces + uppercases.

**Label pairing rule (confirmed):**
  Image: {patient_id}_{class}_{phase}_0000.nii.gz
  Label: {patient_id}_{class}_{phase}.nii.gz
  (strip _0000 from the image filename)

**Volume geometry (CRITICAL for compute plan):**
  Sampled 6 volumes:
  - Contrast phases (C-pre, C+A, C+V, C+Delay), InPhase, T2WI, OutPhase:
    shape (512, 512, 72), spacing ~(0.74, 0.74, 2.6) mm
  - DWI: shape (256, 256, 24), spacing ~(1.48, 1.48, 8.0) mm
  These are FULL-ABDOMEN volumes. Lesion mask is binary 0/1, covers ~0.1% of voxels.
  Action: ROI cropping is essential before any CNN. build_cache.py crops to mask bbox
  + 20% margin per axis, then resamples to 32×64×64, z-scores, saves float16 .npy.

  3984 tensors × 32×64×64 × float16 (2 bytes) = 3984 × 262,144 B ≈ 1.04 GB ✓
